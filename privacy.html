Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026 | Contact: admin@invoicesnapbot.com

The Short Version

Betty reads your receipts, puts the data in a spreadsheet, and keeps a compressed copy in a shared Google Drive folder. We track basic usage stats so we know what's working and what's not. We don't sell your data. We don't share it with advertisers.

What We Collect and Why

Your Account

When you set up, we create:

  • A customer ID (e.g. IS-12345) to link your account across platforms
  • A Google Drive folder on our Google Workspace account, shared with you, containing your spreadsheet and receipt copies
  • We store your email address (to share the folder) and your platform ID (Discord or WhatsApp) to recognise you

Your Receipts

When you send a receipt:

  • We extract the text (vendor, amount, date, category) using AI and add it to your spreadsheet
  • We save a compressed copy of the image to our Google Drive, in a folder shared with you, as a backup for your convenience
  • The original file is processed in memory and not kept on our servers

Your Messages

When you message Betty:

  • We log your messages to understand how people use the service - what features get asked about, what's confusing, what's missing
  • We don't use your messages for advertising or share them with third parties

Usage Data

We track anonymous usage statistics:

  • Which commands get used and how often
  • Receipts processed (count, not content)
  • Which platform you're on (Discord or WhatsApp)
  • Errors and issues (so we can fix them)

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe and PayPal. We never see your full card details. Through our payment dashboards we can see your name, email, card type/last four digits, and payment history.

Selfies

If you use the selfie feature, your scene description is sent to an AI image provider. We don't store the prompt or the generated image after delivery. We track the count only.

If you cancel your paid subscription, your data stays intact and you revert to the free tier. You can request deletion of your account and data at any time by emailing admin@invoicesnapbot.com.

Inactivity

Accounts with no activity for 12 consecutive months — free or paid — are deleted along with all associated data. We'll notify you before this happens so you can export your data or resume using the service.

Your Google Drive Folder

We create a folder on our Google Workspace account ("InvoiceSnap - [your ID]") containing your spreadsheet and receipt copies. You are added as an editor so you can view, export, and share the contents - for example, with your accountant or bookkeeper. The folder is owned by our service account as required by Google Workspace.

Data Security

  • Customer data is encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) and in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Our server sits behind Cloudflare's network
  • Receipt copies are stored in Google Drive, not on our application servers
  • Access to customer data is restricted to the service operator

AI Processing

Betty uses AI services (Google Gemini, Kimi, DeepSeek) to read your receipts and write responses. These services:

  • Receive only the specific receipt or message being processed
  • Do not store your data for training (per their API terms)
  • Have no access to your account, spreadsheet, or other receipts

The AI sees one receipt at a time, does its job, and moves on.

Your Rights

Your receipt data, extracted information, and receipt copies belong to you. You can view, download, share, or distribute any of your data at any time, with anyone you choose, while your account is active. We own the spreadsheet template, code, and service infrastructure - but the data inside is yours. If your account is deleted, you can export a copy of everything beforehand.

You can:

  • View your data - open the shared Google Drive folder anytime
  • Export it - download your spreadsheet and receipt copies from the shared folder
  • Share it - share the folder with your accountant, bookkeeper, or anyone else
  • Delete it - email admin@invoicesnapbot.com and we'll remove your account and all associated data within 7 days
  • Unlink a platform - we can remove Discord or WhatsApp access while keeping your account

Children

InvoiceSnap is not intended for anyone under 18. If we discover a minor is using the service, we'll delete their account.

Changes to This Policy

If we make significant changes, we'll notify users through Betty. Minor clarifications won't trigger a notification.

Questions? Email admin@invoicesnapbot.com.
