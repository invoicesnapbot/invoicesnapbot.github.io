Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026 | Contact: admin@invoicesnapbot.com

The Short Version

Betty reads your receipts, puts the data in your spreadsheet, and keeps a compressed copy in your Google Drive folder. We track basic usage stats so we know what's working and what's not. We don't sell your data. We don't share it with advertisers. If you leave, we delete everything.

What We Collect and Why

Your Account

When you set up, we create:

  • A customer ID (e.g. IS-12345) to link your account across platforms
  • A Google Drive folder shared with you, containing your spreadsheet and receipt copies
  • We store your email address (to share the folder) and your platform ID (Discord or WhatsApp) to recognise you

Your Receipts

When you send a receipt:

  • We extract the text (vendor, amount, date, category) using AI and add it to your spreadsheet
  • We save a compressed copy of the image or document to your Google Drive folder, so you have a digital backup
  • The original file is processed in memory and not kept on our servers

Your Messages

When you message Betty:

  • We log your messages to understand how people use the service - what features get asked about, what's confusing, what's missing
  • This is standard for any chatbot or customer service tool
  • We don't use your messages for advertising or share them with third parties

Usage Data

We track anonymous usage statistics:

  • Which commands get used and how often
  • Receipts processed (count, not content)
  • Which platform you're on (Discord or WhatsApp)
  • Errors and issues (so we can fix them)

This helps us improve Betty. It's the same kind of data any app collects.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe and PayPal. We never see your full card details. Through our payment dashboards we can see your name, email, card type/last four digits, and payment history - standard for any online merchant.

Selfies

If you use the selfie feature, your scene description is sent to an AI image provider. We don't store the prompt or the generated image after delivery. We track the count only.

Your Google Drive Folder

We create a folder in your Google Drive called "InvoiceSnap - [your ID]". It contains your spreadsheet and copies of your receipts. You're added as an editor with full sharing permissions - you can share it with your accountant, bookkeeper, or anyone else. The folder is owned by our service account (a Google Workspace requirement) but the data inside is yours.

Data Security

  • Customer data is encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) and in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Our server sits behind Cloudflare's network
  • Receipt images are compressed and stored in your own Google Drive - not on our servers
  • Access to customer data is restricted to the service operator

AI Processing

Betty uses AI services (Google Gemini, Kimi, DeepSeek) to read your receipts and write responses. These services:

  • Receive only the specific receipt or message being processed
  • Do not store your data for training (per their API terms)
  • Have no access to your account, spreadsheet, or other receipts

The AI sees one receipt at a time, does its job, and moves on.

Your Rights

You can:

  • See your data - it's in your Google Sheet and Drive folder, always accessible
  • Export it - download your spreadsheet anytime
  • Delete it - email admin@invoicesnapbot.com and we'll remove your account and all associated data within 7 days
  • Unlink a platform - we can remove Discord or WhatsApp access while keeping your account

Children

InvoiceSnap is not intended for anyone under 18. If we discover a minor is using the service, we'll delete their account.

Changes to This Policy

If we make significant changes, we'll notify users through Betty. Minor clarifications won't trigger a notification.

Questions? Email admin@invoicesnapbot.com.
