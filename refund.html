Refund Policy

Last updated: March 2026 | Contact: admin@invoicesnapbot.com

Free Tier

InvoiceSnap offers a free tier with 15 receipts per month. No payment is required, so no refund applies. If you're unsure whether InvoiceSnap is right for you, we encourage you to use the free tier first.

Paid Tier - No Refunds After Service Begins

The paid tier ($9/month for unlimited receipts) is a subscription-based digital service that begins immediately upon payment. Once your payment is processed and your account is upgraded, the service is considered active and no refunds are provided for that billing period.

Why No Refunds?

Your $9 monthly subscription covers:

  • Unlimited AI processing of your receipts
  • Continuous access to the extraction service
  • Compressed receipt copies saved to a shared Google Drive folder
  • Personality-driven commentary on your receipts

Because the service starts instantly and cannot be "returned," we do not offer refunds once the service has been delivered.

Before You Pay

We encourage you to:

  • Use the free tier (15 receipts/month) to test the service before upgrading
  • Review Betty's capabilities by typing /help on Discord or "help" on WhatsApp
  • Ensure you have a valid Google account that can accept editor invitations
  • Understand that AI extraction may not be 100% accurate
  • Contact us at admin@invoicesnapbot.com if you have questions before subscribing

Image Generation (Selfies)

Selfies earned through voting or included with your account are non-refundable. If our content filter blocks your prompt, the selfie is still consumed. If an image fails to generate due to a technical error on our end, the selfie is automatically refunded to your account.

Exception - Technical Failure

If a technical failure on our end prevents the service from functioning after successful payment, we will:

  • Attempt to resolve the issue promptly
  • If unresolved within 48 hours of us becoming aware of the issue (excluding circumstances beyond our control, such as power outages, natural disasters, or third-party service outages), provide a full refund

This does not apply to:

  • User error (incorrect Google account, inability to accept editor invitations, etc.)
  • Dissatisfaction with extraction accuracy
  • Change of mind after upgrading
  • Issues caused by Discord, WhatsApp, Google, Stripe, or PayPal outages beyond our control

How to Request a Refund (Technical Failures Only)

Email admin@invoicesnapbot.com with:

  • Your customer ID (e.g. IS-12345) or email address
  • The email address associated with your Stripe or PayPal payment
  • Date and approximate time of payment
  • Description of the issue

We aim to respond within 3 business days.

Chargebacks

Initiating a chargeback without first contacting us violates our Terms of Service and may result in permanent suspension from using InvoiceSnap.

Questions? Email admin@invoicesnapbot.com before upgrading if you're unsure about anything.
