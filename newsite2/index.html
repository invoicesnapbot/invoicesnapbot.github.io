Plain answers

Questions, sorted.

Is this bullshit?
No, it's not. I am 1 bloke (Brendon) + AI (Betty) doing everything. I hate doing finances and paperwork. The AI boom happened and I made an AI to do my paperwork and finances for me. Now it's good enough for me to share and make a side hustle out of it.
Is my data actually mine?
Your data's yours. Betty creates you a spreadsheet of every receipt — yours to open, sort and download whenever. Cancel and you keep every record. We never sell it, share it, or train AI on it. Full stop.
Am I locked into a contract?
Cancel anytime. Message Betty saying CANCEL. That's the cancellation flow. No retention specialist. No 12-month lock-in. No BS.
Is it in a format my accountant can use?
Your sheet matches the categories your accountant already uses. Hand it over at tax time. Done. They'll thank you instead of charging you to type it in.
Is Betty actually a person?
No. Betty's our system - software, doing the reading and sorting. We named her Betty because "AI tax assistant" sounds wanky. There's a real human on the other end if something goes sideways (Brendon, mostly. G'day.).
What if Betty reads a receipt wrong?
She'll ask. If something's unclear - faded ink, weird supplier, ambiguous total - Betty messages you back to confirm. You can also fix any line directly in the Google Sheet. It's your sheet.
Do I need to be tech-savvy?
If you can send a photo on WhatsApp, Discord or email, you can use Betty. That's the whole deal. No app to install, no dashboards, no settings menus you'll never look at twice.
What about my privacy?
Receipts are stored encrypted on AU servers, attached to the spreadsheet Betty keeps for you and shares to your Google account. We don't sell data. We don't train AI on it. We don't share it with anyone - not the ATO, not advertisers, not your nosy cousin who works at Centrelink.
Will my accountant be happy with this?
Yeah - most accountants love it. They get a clean, categorised sheet instead of a shoebox of crumpled paper in July. You can share your spreadsheet with them directly.
How do I cancel?
Message Betty the word CANCEL. That's it. You'll get pro-rata refund of any unused days, and your sheet stays put. No 5-screen unsubscribe flow.
Is this an app I have to download?
No. Betty works over WhatsApp, Discord or email — whatever you already use. Save the contact, send receipts. That's the whole install.
Where do my receipts actually end up?
In a tidy Google Sheet Betty keeps for you and shares straight to your Google account — photos attached to each row. We hold encrypted backups on AU servers. Cancel anytime and you keep every record.
Does Betty replace my accountant?
No, and she's not trying to. She just gets your receipts into one neat spreadsheet so your accountant doesn't charge you to type them in. They'll thank you.
What if a receipt is faded or crumpled?
Crumpled, faded, iced-coffee-stained — Betty will give it a go. If she can't read something specific, she'll message back to confirm before adding it to your sheet. She won't make up numbers.
Is this ATO compliant?
The ATO accepts photographs of receipts as long as they're legible and you can produce them on request. Betty stores the photo alongside the row in your sheet — easy to produce. Your accountant signs off on what gets claimed; Betty doesn't give tax advice.
